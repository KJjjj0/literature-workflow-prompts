# Writing Policy

- Use the approved synthesis as the section structure.
- Use only evidence cards and the evidence matrix for factual claims.
- Every substantive claim must map to at least one corpus paper and bibliography key.
- Distinguish `shows`, `suggests`, `reports`, and `does not establish`.
- Do not describe a preprint as peer-reviewed.
- Do not claim a consensus when the evidence is mixed or sparse.
- Do not add a paper during drafting. Reopen corpus and evidence gates instead.
- Preserve uncertainty around benchmark transfer, data leakage, compute differences, and non-comparable evaluation settings.
- Related Work should synthesize method families and trade-offs instead of listing papers one by one.
