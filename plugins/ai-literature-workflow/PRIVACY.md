# Privacy

The plugin's bundled scripts run locally and do not collect telemetry.

Research queries, candidate records, evidence cards, drafts, citations, and
audit files stay in the project directory selected by the user. The plugin does
not upload them.

Optional external research adapters may send queries or URLs to their
respective providers. Their privacy terms and authentication policies apply.

Do not place unpublished manuscripts, confidential review material, personal
data, or credentials in a directory that will be published or shared.
