# Audit Contract

## Citation Audit

Check:

- every cited bibliography key exists in `references/references.bib`;
- every bibliography key maps to an included corpus row;
- DOI and arXiv identifiers are syntactically valid when present;
- preprint and peer-review status match the evidence card;
- no citation is used only as decoration.

## Consistency Audit

Check:

- title, years, venue, and identifiers agree across corpus, notes, matrix, and bibliography;
- every reported result matches the evidence card;
- claim scope matches the human-approved quality decision;
- research gaps are not contradicted by the included evidence.

## Final Audit

Check:

- citation audit passed;
- consistency audit passed;
- no unresolved placeholders;
- no citations outside the frozen corpus;
- no unsupported contribution or superiority claim;
- venue formatting requirements passed;
- human submission approval is recorded.
